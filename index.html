Our story

Some places are built for buying things.
This one is built for staying.

Picture it: the lamps are glowing, something warm is steaming at the counter, and the whole room is exhaling. A worn walnut table holds a half-finished jigsaw. A shelf of well-loved games leans against a shelf of well-loved books. In the corner, a basket of embroidery thread waits for whoever wants it.

It began as a daydream between two people who wished there were somewhere to simply be together — somewhere that made it easier to step away from screens and back toward each other. So we’re imagining it into being: a gentle, whimsical, a-little-bit-nostalgic space where an afternoon can stretch into an evening, and strangers can become regulars.

Come for the coffee. Stay for the rematch. Leave with a friendship bracelet, a dog-eared paperback, and the feeling that the small things are the big things.

Wi-Fi is here if you need it — but we’ll gently nudge you to leave your phone in your pocket.

The space

Three reasons to linger

Pull up a chair. Every corner of the shop has its own way of slowing you down.

The Board Game Library & Play Tables

Floor-to-ceiling shelves of games you can actually play — no sealed boxes, no hovering. Grab one, claim a big wooden table, and settle in.

  • Board games, card games, and the classics — chess, checkers & more
  • A standing jigsaw table and cooperative games for the team-minded
  • Weekly game nights and friendly tournaments
  • A game-library membership for unlimited play

“Quality time over screen time.”

Thrifted & Curated Books

Every book here has had a past life. We rescue them from estate sales and thrift-store shelves, then give them a second home — and a new reader.

  • Well-loved novels, cookbooks, magazines, and picture books
  • Quiet corners with deep chairs for solo reading
  • Book clubs, including our hushed Sunday silent reading hour
  • Folded-page book art on the walls (yes, really)

Dog-eared pages are a feature.

Community Crafts & Makers’ Corner

A cozy bench stocked with paints, thread, and paper — for anyone who’s ever said, “I should make something.” Now’s your chance.

  • DIY craft stations: friendship bracelets, painting, embroidery & more
  • Journaling and scrapbooking supplies by the table
  • Guided workshops and seasonal crafts with local instructors
  • Original photography and local art on the walls, with prints to take home

Pay-per-project, from about $8.
